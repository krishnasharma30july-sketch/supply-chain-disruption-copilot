# Compensation Policy

Compensation is computed deterministically from the number of calendar days an order is late after mitigation. Customer communications must never promise more than this policy allows.

## Compensation tiers

- 1 to 3 days late (minor delay): refund the freight paid for the order.
- 4 to 7 days late (moderate delay): refund the freight, plus a voucher worth 10% of the item value, capped at R$ 50.
- 8 or more days late (major delay): refund the freight, plus a voucher worth 15% of the item value, capped at R$ 100.

Vouchers are single-use, valid for 90 days and issued with a unique code.

## Cancellation and refunds

When a cancellation is offered, the customer may cancel without any fee and receives a full refund of the items and freight to the original payment method. Proactive cancellations (perishable goods) are refunded automatically.

## Critical cases

Orders scored as critical severity also get a personal follow-up from the customer care team (human callback), on top of the standard compensation.

## What we never promise

- Exact delivery dates for disrupted shipments: always give a delivery window.
- Compensation above the tiers in this policy.
- Statements blaming a named carrier, partner or public authority.
