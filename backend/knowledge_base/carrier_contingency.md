# Carrier Contingency Plan

## Disruption types

- Weather (heavy rain, floods, storms): usually regional; roads reopen in stages.
- Strikes (truckers, couriers): can be regional or nationwide and affect road freight. Air corridors stay available unless stated otherwise.
- Infrastructure (road closures, bridge collapses, port issues): local and lane-specific.
- Capacity (volume spikes, backlog): slows every lane through the affected region.
- Supplier or seller outages: affect handover at origin only.

## Recovery

When a disruption ends, the network needs roughly 30% of the disruption's length to clear the backlog. Plan customer delivery windows to include this recovery time.

## Operational levers

- Pre-book alternate carrier or air capacity for parcel-sized shipments blocked at origin.
- Ask affected sellers to hold handovers instead of sending freight into a blocked region.
- Prioritise critical-severity orders for manual follow-up.
- Move eligible orders to alternate sellers outside the disrupted area.
