# Product Handling Guide

## Handling profiles

Every product line has a handling profile derived from its billable weight (the larger of actual weight and volumetric weight, L x W x H / 6000):
- Parcel: up to 5 kg billable and up to 60 cm on the longest side. Eligible for alternate carriers and air corridors.
- Medium: up to 30 kg billable. Road freight only.
- Bulky: over 30 kg billable, any side over 100 cm, or a combined size over 200 cm. Needs dedicated road freight, often a two-person delivery.

## Product classes

- Perishable: food, beverages, flowers. Time-critical; spoils if delayed.
- Essential: baby, hygiene, health and pet supplies. Customers often depend on them.
- Fragile: musical instruments, cameras, kitchenware, art. Avoid extra handling such as transfers between carriers.
- High-value tech: computers, phones, consoles, audio. Higher theft risk; prefer insured corridors.
- Gift and seasonal: watches, gifts, toys, party and Christmas items. Bought for a date.
- Furniture and home: furniture, home goods, appliances, construction and garden. Often bulky.
- Standard: everything else.

## Heavy and bulky goods during disruptions

Heavy and bulky freight recovers slowest after a disruption because it needs dedicated trucks. Expect the backlog to clear after parcel traffic does.
